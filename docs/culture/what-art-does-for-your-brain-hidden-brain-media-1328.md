# What Art Does For Your Brain - Hidden Brain Media

Think about the last time a piece of music stopped you in your tracks, or a novel kept you up late at night. Why do these experiences feel so powerful? Psychobiologist [Daisy Fancourt](https://profiles.ucl.ac.uk/44526-daisy-fancourt) says engagement with the arts can help to regulate our emotions, build resilience, and support long-term well-being. She joins us to talk about this research, and to share practical ways to bring more creativity into our everyday lives.
