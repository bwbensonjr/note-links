**U.S. Route 20** or **U.S. Highway 20** (**US 20**) is an east–west [United States Numbered Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Numbered_Highway_System "United States Numbered Highway System"), which stretches from the [Pacific Northwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Northwest "Pacific Northwest") east to [New England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_England "New England"). The "0" in its route number indicates that US 20 is a major coast-to-coast route. Spanning 3,254.49 miles (5,237.59 km), it is the longest road in the United States,[[2]](#cite_note-fhwa-2) and, in the east, the route is roughly parallel to [Interstate 90](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_90 "Interstate 90") (I-90), which is the longest [Interstate Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_Highway_System "Interstate Highway System") in the U.S. There is a discontinuity in the official designation of US 20 through [Yellowstone National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_National_Park "Yellowstone National Park"), with unnumbered roads used to traverse the park.

US 20 and [US 30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_30 "U.S. Route 30") break the general U.S. Route numbering rules in [Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon "Oregon"), since US 30 actually starts north of US 20 in [Astoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astoria,_Oregon "Astoria, Oregon"), and runs parallel to the north throughout the state (the [Columbia River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_River "Columbia River") and [Interstate 84](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_84_(Oregon–Utah) "Interstate 84 (Oregon–Utah)")). The two [run concurrently](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concurrency_(road) "Concurrency (road)") and continue in the correct positioning near [Caldwell, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caldwell,_Idaho "Caldwell, Idaho"). This is because US 20 was not a planned coast-to-coast route while US 30 was. US 20 originally ended at the eastern entrance of Yellowstone Park; it was extended in 1940.[[2]](#cite_note-fhwa-2)

The highway's eastern terminus is in [Boston, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston "Boston"), at [Kenmore Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenmore_Square "Kenmore Square"), where it meets [Massachusetts Route 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Route_2 "Massachusetts Route 2"). Its western terminus is in [Newport, Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newport,_Oregon "Newport, Oregon"), at an intersection with [US 101](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_101 "U.S. Route 101"), within a mile of the [Pacific Ocean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Ocean "Pacific Ocean").[[3]](#cite_note-termini-3)

### Oregon

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=U.S._Route_20&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Oregon")]

Main article: [U.S. Route 20 in Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_20_in_Oregon "U.S. Route 20 in Oregon")

[![](//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a7/20East.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail_unscaled)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:20East.jpg)

Sign at western origin in Newport, Oregon

US 20 begins at an intersection with [US 101](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_101_(Oregon) "U.S. Route 101 (Oregon)") in [Newport, Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newport,_Oregon "Newport, Oregon"), and runs generally eastward towards [Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho "Idaho"). On the way it goes over the [Central Oregon Coast Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Oregon_Coast_Range "Central Oregon Coast Range"), through several [Willamette Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willamette_Valley "Willamette Valley") cities including [Corvallis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corvallis,_Oregon "Corvallis, Oregon") and [Albany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albany,_Oregon "Albany, Oregon"), climbs the [Cascade Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cascade_Range "Cascade Range") over [Santiam Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiam_Pass "Santiam Pass"), goes through [Bend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bend,_Oregon "Bend, Oregon"), and traverses the [Oregon High Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Desert_(Oregon) "High Desert (Oregon)") after passing through [Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burns,_Oregon "Burns, Oregon"). It eventually overlaps [US 26](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_26_in_Oregon "U.S. Route 26 in Oregon") in [Vale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vale,_Oregon "Vale, Oregon"), and the two highways continue concurrently to the [Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho "Idaho") border.

### Idaho

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=U.S._Route_20&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Idaho")]

Main article: [U.S. Route 20 in Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_20_in_Idaho "U.S. Route 20 in Idaho")

US 20 crosses into Idaho from Oregon northwest of [Parma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parma,_Idaho "Parma, Idaho"). It [runs concurrently](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concurrency_(road) "Concurrency (road)") with [US 26](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_26 "U.S. Route 26") and joins [US 95](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_95 "U.S. Route 95") through Parma. US 20/US 26 leaves US 95 southeast of Parma and runs to [Caldwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caldwell,_Idaho "Caldwell, Idaho") where US 20/US 26 joins with [I-84](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_84_in_Idaho "Interstate 84 in Idaho") and [US 30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_30_in_Idaho "U.S. Route 30 in Idaho") for a short time. These four highways parallel each other (on two roadways) to [Boise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho "Boise, Idaho") where US 20/US 26 runs through downtown before joining with I-84 and US 30 again to [Mountain Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_Home,_Idaho "Mountain Home, Idaho"), where it departs at exit 95 to head east, past [Rattlesnake Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rattlesnake_Station "Rattlesnake Station"), [Anderson Ranch Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anderson_Ranch_Dam "Anderson Ranch Dam") road, and cresting at Cat Creek summit at 5,527 feet (1,685 m) [above mean sea level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Above_mean_sea_level "Above mean sea level"). It continues into and across [Camas County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camas_County,_Idaho "Camas County, Idaho") through [Fairfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairfield,_Idaho "Fairfield, Idaho") to Timmerman Junction, the intersection in [Blaine County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blaine_County,_Idaho "Blaine County, Idaho") with [State Highway 75](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho_State_Highway_75 "Idaho State Highway 75"), the route to [Sun Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Valley,_Idaho "Sun Valley, Idaho"), [Galena Summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galena_Summit "Galena Summit"), and [Stanley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley,_Idaho "Stanley, Idaho"). US 20 continues east through [Picabo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Picabo,_Idaho "Picabo, Idaho") and [Carey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carey,_Idaho "Carey, Idaho"), joined with US 26 and [US 93](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_93 "U.S. Route 93"), to [Craters of the Moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craters_of_the_Moon_National_Monument_and_Preserve "Craters of the Moon National Monument and Preserve") and [Arco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arco,_Idaho "Arco, Idaho"), where US 93 splits off and turns north-northwest to climb the [Big Lost River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Lost_River "Big Lost River") valley. US 20/US 26 continues on through the [Idaho National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho_National_Laboratory "Idaho National Laboratory"), where the highways split just west of [Atomic City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_City,_Idaho "Atomic City, Idaho"); US 26 heads to [Blackfoot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackfoot,_Idaho "Blackfoot, Idaho") and US 20 to [Idaho Falls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho_Falls,_Idaho "Idaho Falls, Idaho"), where it turns north-northeast to pass near [Rexburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rexburg,_Idaho "Rexburg, Idaho") as a freeway. US 20 then climbs through the communities of [St. Anthony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Anthony,_Idaho "St. Anthony, Idaho"), [Ashton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashton,_Idaho "Ashton, Idaho"), and [Island Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island_Park,_Idaho "Island Park, Idaho"), and crosses the [Continental Divide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_Divide_of_the_Americas "Continental Divide of the Americas") at [Targhee Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Targhee_Pass "Targhee Pass") at 7,072 feet (2,156 m), entering [Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montana "Montana") west of [West Yellowstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Yellowstone,_Montana "West Yellowstone, Montana").

### Montana

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=U.S._Route_20&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Montana")]

Main article: [U.S. Route 20 in Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_20_in_Montana "U.S. Route 20 in Montana")

In the state of Montana, US 20 runs for less than 10 miles (16 km). It runs from the Idaho state line to [West Yellowstone, Montana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Yellowstone,_Montana "West Yellowstone, Montana"), the western entrance to [Yellowstone National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_National_Park "Yellowstone National Park"). US 20 is known as the [Targhee Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Targhee_Pass "Targhee Pass") Highway in Montana.

While US 20 and other U.S. Routes are officially discontinuous through the park, some commercially produced maps show these highways running inside Yellowstone National Park itself along its unnumbered roads and across the Montana–Wyoming state line.

### Wyoming

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=U.S._Route_20&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Wyoming")]

Main article: [U.S. Route 20 in Wyoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_20_in_Wyoming "U.S. Route 20 in Wyoming")

In the state of Wyoming, the eastern segment of US 20 starts at the eastern entrance to [Yellowstone National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_National_Park "Yellowstone National Park") along with the western termini of [US 14](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Highway_14_(Wyoming) "U.S. Highway 14 (Wyoming)") and [US 16](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Highway_16_(Wyoming) "U.S. Highway 16 (Wyoming)"). These three routes run east to [Greybull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greybull,_Wyoming "Greybull, Wyoming"), where US 14 continues east and US 16/US 20 turns south; at [Worland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worland,_Wyoming "Worland, Wyoming"), US 16 turns east while US 20 continues south, passing through [Wind River Canyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_River_Canyon "Wind River Canyon") south of [Thermopolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermopolis,_Wyoming "Thermopolis, Wyoming"). US 20 joins US 26 in [Shoshoni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoshoni,_Wyoming "Shoshoni, Wyoming"), where it turns east, and they continue all the way through [Casper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casper,_Wyoming "Casper, Wyoming"). From Casper, the two highways parallel [I-25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_25_(Wyoming) "Interstate 25 (Wyoming)") and [US 87](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Highway_87_(Wyoming) "U.S. Highway 87 (Wyoming)") for 26 miles, until all four link up together just southeast of [Glenrock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenrock,_Wyoming "Glenrock, Wyoming"). These four routes stay combined to [Orin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orin,_Wyoming "Orin, Wyoming"), where US 20 turns east from I-25, at the western end of [US 18](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Highway_18_(Wyoming) "U.S. Highway 18 (Wyoming)"). US 18 and US 20 are concurrent from Orin to [Lusk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lusk,_Wyoming "Lusk, Wyoming"), where US 18 turns north and US 20 continues east into Nebraska.

### Nebraska

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=U.S._Route_20&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Nebraska")]

Main article: [U.S. Route 20 in Nebraska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_20_in_Nebraska "U.S. Route 20 in Nebraska")

See also: [Interstate 129](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_129 "Interstate 129")

[![](//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/2c/EnteringNebraska.jpg/250px-EnteringNebraska.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:EnteringNebraska.jpg)

US 20 enters Nebraska from Wyoming

Within [Nebraska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nebraska "Nebraska"), US 20 is a [state highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nebraska_State_Highway_System "Nebraska State Highway System") that begins on the [Wyoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wyoming "Wyoming")–Nebraska state line west of [Harrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrison,_Nebraska "Harrison, Nebraska") near the [Niobrara River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niobrara_River "Niobrara River") and runs to the Nebraska–[Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa "Iowa") state line in [South Sioux City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Sioux_City,_Nebraska "South Sioux City, Nebraska"). Throughout its 431.60-mile (694.59 km) length the route passes through a diverse range of landscapes including [bluffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beach_ridge "Beach ridge") and [escarpments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escarpment "Escarpment") in the Northwest [Panhandle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nebraska_Panhandle "Nebraska Panhandle"), the [Nebraska Sandhills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nebraska_Sandhills "Nebraska Sandhills") in the northern part of the state, and rolling hills and plains through Randolph, Nebraska as the highway approaches the [Missouri River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri_River "Missouri River") valley south of [Sioux City, Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sioux_City,_Iowa "Sioux City, Iowa").[[4]](#cite_note-unltopmap-4)[[5]](#cite_note-5) Throughout its length, US 20 is a two-lane highway with the exception of the easternmost 8.45 miles (13.60 km) which is four-lane divided highway, the last 3.21 miles (5.17 km) of which is concurrent with [Interstate 129](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_129 "Interstate 129").[[6]](#cite_note-ndor-6)

### Iowa

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=U.S._Route_20&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Iowa")]

Main article: [U.S. Route 20 in Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_20_in_Iowa "U.S. Route 20 in Iowa")

[![](//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d8/Dubuque_Iowa_Julien_Dubuque_Bridge.jpg/250px-Dubuque_Iowa_Julien_Dubuque_Bridge.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Dubuque_Iowa_Julien_Dubuque_Bridge.jpg)

US 20 crosses the Mississippi River connecting Dubuque, Iowa, and East Dubuque, Illinois

US 20 enters Iowa at [Sioux City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sioux_City,_Iowa "Sioux City, Iowa") via the [Missouri River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri_River "Missouri River") crossing with [I-129](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_129 "Interstate 129") and [US 75](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_75 "U.S. Route 75"). After skirting the southeast side of Sioux City as a freeway with US 75, US 20 continues east as an [expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limited-access_road "Limited-access road") to [Moville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moville,_Iowa "Moville, Iowa"). From Moville through north of Early at the junction with U.S. Route 71 and [Iowa Highway 471](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_Highway_471 "Iowa Highway 471"), US 20 was reconstructed from a rural two-lane highway to a four-lane road. This segment re-opened October 19, 2018 and made it so that US 20 is a continuous four-lane highway for its entire length in Iowa. It passes to the north of [Sac City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sac_City,_Iowa "Sac City, Iowa"), where it has another interchange with the realigned [U.S. Route 71](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_71 "U.S. Route 71"), then passes to the south of [Fort Dodge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Dodge,_Iowa "Fort Dodge, Iowa") and [Webster City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Webster_City,_Iowa "Webster City, Iowa") before intersecting [I-35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_35 "Interstate 35") near [Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams,_Iowa "Williams, Iowa").

A new segment of freeway between [US 65](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_65 "U.S. Route 65") south of [Iowa Falls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_Falls,_Iowa "Iowa Falls, Iowa") and [Iowa Highway 14](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_Highway_14 "Iowa Highway 14") opened in 2003 creating a continuous four-lane route from Moorland to Dubuque. The new segment shaved 16 miles (26 km) off US 20's length in Iowa. In the [Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterloo,_Iowa "Waterloo, Iowa")/[Cedar Falls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cedar_Falls,_Iowa "Cedar Falls, Iowa") area, the segment of US 20 overlapped by the [Avenue of the Saints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avenue_of_the_Saints "Avenue of the Saints"), which is also designated as Iowa Highway 27. US 20 passes [Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence,_Iowa "Independence, Iowa"), [Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester,_Iowa "Manchester, Iowa"), and [Dyersville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyersville,_Iowa "Dyersville, Iowa") before reaching [Dubuque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubuque,_Iowa "Dubuque, Iowa"). At Dubuque, US 20 crosses into Illinois over the [Julien Dubuque Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julien_Dubuque_Bridge "Julien Dubuque Bridge").

### Illinois

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=U.S._Route_20&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Illinois")]

Main article: [U.S. Route 20 in Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_20_in_Illinois "U.S. Route 20 in Illinois")

See also: [Interstate 290 (Illinois)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_290_(Illinois) "Interstate 290 (Illinois)"), [North Avenue (Chicago)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Avenue_(Chicago) "North Avenue (Chicago)"), [Lake Street (Chicago)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Street_(Chicago) "Lake Street (Chicago)"), [Mannheim Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mannheim_Road "Mannheim Road"), and [95th Street (Chicago)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/95th_Street_(Chicago) "95th Street (Chicago)")

In the state of Illinois, US 20 begins in [East Dubuque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Dubuque,_Illinois "East Dubuque, Illinois"), following southeastward along the Mississippi River, and continues into the very hilly [Driftless Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Driftless_Area "Driftless Area") of northwest Illinois through [Galena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galena,_Illinois "Galena, Illinois") and [Elizabeth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth,_Illinois "Elizabeth, Illinois"). The highway then transitions eastward from the Driftless Area to the Interior Plains near [Stockton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockton,_Illinois "Stockton, Illinois"). The road continues as a bypass north of [Freeport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freeport,_Illinois "Freeport, Illinois"), and then runs as a freeway along the southern fringe of [Rockford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockford,_Illinois "Rockford, Illinois"). From Rockford to [Chicago, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago,_Illinois "Chicago, Illinois"), US 20 is a mixture of four-lane expressway, four-lane limited access freeway, and winding two-lane surface road. It is signed as "General U.S. Grant Highway" after [Ulysses S. Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulysses_S._Grant "Ulysses S. Grant"). U.S. 20 runs through the south side of Chicago as 95th Street. US 20 exits Illinois along with [US 12](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_12 "U.S. Route 12") and [US 41](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_41_in_Illinois "U.S. Route 41 in Illinois") as Indianapolis Boulevard south of the [Chicago Skyway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Skyway "Chicago Skyway").

### Indiana

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=U.S._Route_20&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Indiana")]

Main article: [U.S. Route 20 in Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_20_in_Indiana "U.S. Route 20 in Indiana")

See also: [St. Joseph Valley Parkway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Joseph_Valley_Parkway "St. Joseph Valley Parkway")

[![](//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/81/Angola-indiana-panorama.jpg/250px-Angola-indiana-panorama.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Angola-indiana-panorama.jpg)

Looking eastbound at US 20 as it meets State Road 127 in a traffic circle in Angola, Indiana

In the state of Indiana, US 20 enters from the west beneath the [Chicago Skyway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Skyway "Chicago Skyway") with US 12 and US 41. US 20 passes through heavily industrialized northwestern Indiana and is the main east–west artery through [Gary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary,_Indiana "Gary, Indiana"). It then parallels US 12 to just west of [Michigan City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_City,_Indiana "Michigan City, Indiana") before running due east through [New Carlisle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Carlisle,_Indiana "New Carlisle, Indiana") to [South Bend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Bend,_Indiana "South Bend, Indiana"). US 20 travels around South Bend and [Elkhart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elkhart,_Indiana "Elkhart, Indiana") on the [St. Joseph Valley Parkway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Joseph_Valley_Parkway "St. Joseph Valley Parkway"), a four-lane limited access freeway, then returns to surface road east of Elkhart. US 20 then passes through [LaGrange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LaGrange,_Indiana "LaGrange, Indiana") and [Angola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angola,_Indiana "Angola, Indiana"), intersecting with [I-69](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_69_in_Indiana "Interstate 69 in Indiana") before leaving Indiana just north of the eastern terminus of the [Indiana Toll Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_Toll_Road "Indiana Toll Road").

From [Gary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary,_Indiana "Gary, Indiana") to [South Bend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Bend,_Indiana "South Bend, Indiana"), US 20 was built as the Dunes Relief Road. During the 1930s and 1940s the Dunes Highway, [US 12](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_12 "U.S. Route 12"), was becoming more crowded as housing lots and communities developed in the Indiana Dunes. Today, there are numerous communities along US 20 and the lakefront, including Gary, [Portage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portage,_Indiana "Portage, Indiana"), [Burns Harbor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burns_Harbor,_Indiana "Burns Harbor, Indiana"), [Porter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porter,_Indiana "Porter, Indiana"), [Chesterton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chesterton,_Indiana "Chesterton, Indiana"), [Pines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town_of_Pines,_Indiana "Town of Pines, Indiana"), and [Michigan City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_City,_Indiana "Michigan City, Indiana").

Most notably, the route is one of the main access roads to [Indiana Dunes National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_Dunes_National_Park "Indiana Dunes National Park").

### Ohio

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=U.S._Route_20&action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Ohio")]

Main article: [U.S. Route 20 in Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_20_in_Ohio "U.S. Route 20 in Ohio")

[![](//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a2/U.S._Route_20_and_Ohio_State_Highway_7_Sign.jpg/250px-U.S._Route_20_and_Ohio_State_Highway_7_Sign.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:U.S._Route_20_and_Ohio_State_Highway_7_Sign.jpg)

Sign for US 20 along with the sign for State Route 7 in Conneaut, Ohio

US 20 enters [Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio "Ohio") from Indiana just west of [Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwest_Township,_Williams_County,_Ohio "Northwest Township, Williams County, Ohio") and traverses east through [Pioneer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pioneer,_Ohio "Pioneer, Ohio") and [Alvordton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alvordton,_Ohio "Alvordton, Ohio") to [Fayette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fayette,_Ohio "Fayette, Ohio"). It continues east through [Oakshade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chesterfield_Township,_Ohio "Chesterfield Township, Ohio"), [Assumption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assumption,_Ohio "Assumption, Ohio") and becomes five-lane, non-limited access roadway into [Sylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvania,_Ohio "Sylvania, Ohio") to [Toledo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo,_Ohio "Toledo, Ohio"), and then turns south until into the Toledo suburb of [Maumee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maumee,_Ohio "Maumee, Ohio"). After Maumee, US 20 crosses Maumee River curving back east becoming 2 lanes through [Perrysburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perrysburg,_Ohio "Perrysburg, Ohio") to [Woodville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodville,_Ohio "Woodville, Ohio"), where it becomes a four-lane, non-limited access highway. It bypasses [Fremont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fremont,_Ohio "Fremont, Ohio") while concurrent with [US 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_6 "U.S. Route 6"), [State Route 53](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Route_53_(Ohio) "State Route 53 (Ohio)"), and [State Route 19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Route_19_(Ohio) "State Route 19 (Ohio)"), and then travels through the northern edge of [Clyde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clyde,_Ohio "Clyde, Ohio") and the downtown area of [Bellevue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bellevue,_Ohio "Bellevue, Ohio"), where it is joined by [State Route 18](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Route_18_(Ohio) "State Route 18 (Ohio)"). From there the four-lane highway continues through [Monroeville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monroeville,_Ohio "Monroeville, Ohio") and bypasses [Norwalk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwalk,_Ohio "Norwalk, Ohio"), then becomes a two-lane highway from there to [Oberlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberlin,_Ohio "Oberlin, Ohio"). Continuing east, US 20 traverses through [Elyria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elyria,_Ohio "Elyria, Ohio"), [North Ridgeville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Ridgeville,_Ohio "North Ridgeville, Ohio"), [Westlake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westlake,_Ohio "Westlake, Ohio"), [Rocky River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky_River,_Ohio "Rocky River, Ohio"), [Lakewood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakewood,_Ohio "Lakewood, Ohio") and [Cleveland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleveland "Cleveland"), where it goes right through [Public Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Square,_Cleveland "Public Square, Cleveland"). East of Cleveland US 20 follows the southern shore of [Lake Erie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Erie "Lake Erie"), following [Euclid Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclid_Avenue_(Cleveland,_Ohio) "Euclid Avenue (Cleveland, Ohio)") from Public Square, and traverses [Euclid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclid,_Ohio "Euclid, Ohio"), [Wickliffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wickliffe,_Ohio "Wickliffe, Ohio"), where it intersects with I-90, [Mentor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mentor,_Ohio "Mentor, Ohio"), [Painesville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Painesville,_Ohio "Painesville, Ohio"), [Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison,_Ohio "Madison, Ohio"), [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva,_Ohio "Geneva, Ohio"), [Ashtabula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashtabula,_Ohio "Ashtabula, Ohio") and crosses into [Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania "Pennsylvania") at [Conneaut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conneaut,_Ohio "Conneaut, Ohio").

### Pennsylvania

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=U.S._Route_20&action=edit&section=11 "Edit section: Pennsylvania")]

Main article: [U.S. Route 20 in Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_20_in_Pennsylvania "U.S. Route 20 in Pennsylvania")

[![](//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5c/I-79_terminus.jpg/250px-I-79_terminus.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:I-79_terminus.jpg)

I-79 at US 20 (exit 182) in Erie, Pennsylvania

US 20 runs for nearly 50 miles (80 km) across [Erie County, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erie_County,_Pennsylvania "Erie County, Pennsylvania"), most of that time just south of the [CSX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSX_Transportation "CSX Transportation")/[Amtrak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amtrak "Amtrak") railroad tracks. US 20 appears as West Ridge Road eastward from the Ohio border through [Springfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springfield_Township,_Erie_County,_Pennsylvania "Springfield Township, Erie County, Pennsylvania"), [Girard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girard_Township,_Erie_County,_Pennsylvania "Girard Township, Erie County, Pennsylvania"), [Fairview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairview_Township,_Erie_County,_Pennsylvania "Fairview Township, Erie County, Pennsylvania") and [Millcreek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millcreek_Township,_Erie_County,_Pennsylvania "Millcreek Township, Erie County, Pennsylvania") townships. At the intersection with [Pennsylvania Route 832](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_Route_832 "Pennsylvania Route 832"), just outside the [Erie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erie,_Pennsylvania "Erie, Pennsylvania") city limits, it becomes West 26th Street. After it reaches city center at the intersection with State Street, it becomes East 26th Street. It turns sharply northward as Broad Street at the [Bayfront Connector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayfront_Connector "Bayfront Connector"), then turns east again as Buffalo Road through [Wesleyville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesleyville,_Pennsylvania "Wesleyville, Pennsylvania") and [Harborcreek Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harborcreek_Township,_Erie_County,_Pennsylvania "Harborcreek Township, Erie County, Pennsylvania"). Buffalo Road turns sharply northward at the town of Harborcreek. US 20 intersects with Pennsylvania Route 955 and resumes its eastward journey. At [North East Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_East_Township,_Erie_County,_Pennsylvania "North East Township, Erie County, Pennsylvania"), US 20 becomes West Main Road. Within the town limits, it becomes West Main Street until it reaches the town center at its intersection with Pennsylvania Route 89, where it becomes East Main Street. Outside the town limits it becomes East Main Road until it reaches Pennsylvania's border with [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_(state) "New York (state)").

### New York

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=U.S._Route_20&action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: New York")]

Main article: [U.S. Route 20 in New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_20_in_New_York "U.S. Route 20 in New York")

[![](//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e5/US20SkaneatelesNY.JPG/250px-US20SkaneatelesNY.JPG?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:US20SkaneatelesNY.JPG)

US 20 marker, east of Sangerfield, New York

In [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_(state) "New York (state)"), US 20 runs roughly parallel to the [New York State Thruway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_State_Thruway "New York State Thruway") (I-90) throughout the state. The route runs concurrently with [New York State Route 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_State_Route_5 "New York State Route 5") twice, with the second overlap extending 68 miles (109 km) across [Western](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_New_York "Western New York") and [Central New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_New_York "Central New York") from [Avon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avon_(town),_New_York "Avon (town), New York") to [Auburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn,_New_York "Auburn, New York") running along the top of the [Finger Lakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finger_Lakes "Finger Lakes") crossing [Canandaigua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canandaigua,_New_York "Canandaigua, New York") and [Seneca Falls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seneca_Falls,_New_York "Seneca Falls, New York") along the way. US 20 runs across the rural [Cherry Valley Turnpike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherry_Valley_Turnpike "Cherry Valley Turnpike") between [Skaneateles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skaneateles_(town),_New_York "Skaneateles (town), New York") and [Albany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albany,_New_York "Albany, New York"). US 20 was also known as the Boston-Buffalo highway prior to the construction of the New York Thruway and Massachusetts Turnpike.

### Massachusetts

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=U.S._Route_20&action=edit&section=13 "Edit section: Massachusetts")]

Main article: [U.S. Route 20 in Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_20_in_Massachusetts "U.S. Route 20 in Massachusetts")

[![](//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4d/Route-20-Sign-Kenmore-Square-December-8-2016.jpg/250px-Route-20-Sign-Kenmore-Square-December-8-2016.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Route-20-Sign-Kenmore-Square-December-8-2016.jpg)

Sign for [Route 20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_20_in_Massachusetts "U.S. Route 20 in Massachusetts") at [Kenmore Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenmore_Square "Kenmore Square"), near the [Boston Citgo sign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Citgo_sign "Boston Citgo sign")

In Massachusetts, US 20 runs roughly parallel to I-90 [Mass Pike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_Pike "Mass Pike"), intersecting it directly at exit 10 and indirectly at exit 94.

In part of [Berkshire County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkshire_County,_Massachusetts "Berkshire County, Massachusetts"), US 20 runs north–south as it runs concurrently with [US 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_7 "U.S. Route 7"). It is known as the "Jacob's Ladder Trail" as it crosses the [Berkshire Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Berkshires "The Berkshires") between [Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee,_Massachusetts "Lee, Massachusetts") in Berkshire County and [Chester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chester,_Massachusetts "Chester, Massachusetts") in [Hampden County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampden_County,_Massachusetts "Hampden County, Massachusetts"). From Chester to [Westfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westfield,_Massachusetts "Westfield, Massachusetts"), US 20 and the [Boston and Albany Railroad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_and_Albany_Railroad "Boston and Albany Railroad") follow the [Westfield River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westfield_River "Westfield River") down to the broad valley of the [Connecticut River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connecticut_River "Connecticut River").

In [Springfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springfield,_Massachusetts "Springfield, Massachusetts"), US 20 runs concurrently with [I-291](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_291_(Massachusetts) "Interstate 291 (Massachusetts)") between Plainfield Street and Page Boulevard; it follows Boston Road as it leaves Springfield and travels through the town of [Wilbraham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilbraham,_Massachusetts "Wilbraham, Massachusetts"), eventually becoming Main Street in [Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palmer,_Massachusetts "Palmer, Massachusetts").

In [Shrewsbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrewsbury,_Massachusetts "Shrewsbury, Massachusetts"), US 20 is called "Hartford Turnpike". In [Northborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northborough,_Massachusetts "Northborough, Massachusetts"), and [Worcester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester,_Massachusetts "Worcester, Massachusetts") it is called "Southwest Cutoff", until it merges with the original [Boston Post Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Post_Road "Boston Post Road") in Northborough (Southwest Cutoff and West Main Street). Southwest Cutoff was created at the turn of the century to bypass truck traffic around Shrewsbury center as well as connecting to the [Boston and Worcester Turnpike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Route_9 "Massachusetts Route 9").

In parts of eastern Massachusetts, US 20 follows the route of the old [Boston Post Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Post_Road "Boston Post Road") and passes by [Longfellow's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Wadsworth_Longfellow "Henry Wadsworth Longfellow") [Wayside Inn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayside_Inn "Wayside Inn"), in [Sudbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudbury,_Massachusetts "Sudbury, Massachusetts"), the oldest continually operated Inn in America. In 1926, after engineers determined that heavy truck traffic on the Boston Post Road was damaging the foundations of the Inn, [Henry Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Ford "Henry Ford"), then owner and proprietor, ordered the construction of the mile-and-a-half-long Route 20 bypass. Upon its completion on December 11, 1928, he sold it to the Commonwealth of Massachusetts for $1 and never cashed the check. According to the [Boston Herald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Herald "Boston Herald") the by-pass cost Ford $288,000, equivalent to roughly $4.7 million in 2021.[[7]](#cite_note-7) The original route of US 20 is now called Wayside Inn Road.

US 20 continues from Sudbury through [Wayland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayland,_Massachusetts "Wayland, Massachusetts"), [Weston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weston,_Massachusetts "Weston, Massachusetts"), [Waltham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waltham,_Massachusetts "Waltham, Massachusetts"), [Watertown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watertown,_Massachusetts "Watertown, Massachusetts") and then into [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston,_Massachusetts "Boston, Massachusetts") through [Allston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allston "Allston") and [Fenway–Kenmore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fenway–Kenmore "Fenway–Kenmore"). U.S. Route 20's official eastern terminus is in [Kenmore Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenmore_Square "Kenmore Square") at the intersection of [Commonwealth Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_Avenue_(Boston) "Commonwealth Avenue (Boston)"), [Beacon Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beacon_Street "Beacon Street"), and [Brookline Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brookline_Avenue "Brookline Avenue"), where the alignment becomes [Massachusetts Route 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Route_2 "Massachusetts Route 2"). Route 2 continues for a short distance where it terminates at [Massachusetts Route 28](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Route_28 "Massachusetts Route 28").

History
-------

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=U.S._Route_20&action=edit&section=14 "Edit section: History")]

[![](//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/41/New_England_5.svg/120px-New_England_5.svg.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:New_England_5.svg)

Before the U.S. Highway System, the route in New England and New York was designated as New England Interstate Route 5 (NE-5), part of the [New England Interstate Route](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_England_Interstate_Route "New England Interstate Route") system that existed between 1922 and 1927. Around 1923, from Springfield to Pittsfield, NE-5 was known as "Jacob's Ladder",[[8]](#cite_note-8) and from Boston to Albany, the "Hubway". When US 20 was first commissioned, it took over the entirety of NE-5.

### Historic termini

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=U.S._Route_20&action=edit&section=15 "Edit section: Historic termini")]

Until 1940, the western endpoint of US 20 was the east entrance to [Yellowstone National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_National_Park "Yellowstone National Park").

### Historic routes

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=U.S._Route_20&action=edit&section=16 "Edit section: Historic routes")]

Between [Fort Dodge, Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Dodge,_Iowa "Fort Dodge, Iowa"), and [Dubuque, Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubuque,_Iowa "Dubuque, Iowa"), the route has been widened to four lanes, the last link of this completed in 2003. Much of the old route is a few miles north of the present route except between [I-35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_35 "Interstate 35") and [Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterloo,_Iowa "Waterloo, Iowa"). There, the old route is farther north, going through [Iowa Falls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_Falls,_Iowa "Iowa Falls, Iowa"), [Aplington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aplington,_Iowa "Aplington, Iowa"), and the north side of [Cedar Falls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cedar_Falls,_Iowa "Cedar Falls, Iowa") along what is now [Iowa Highway 57](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_Highway_57 "Iowa Highway 57").

From just west of [South Bend, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Bend,_Indiana "South Bend, Indiana"), to the St. Joseph – Elkhart county line, the old route of US 20 through [St. Joseph County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Joseph_County,_Indiana "St. Joseph County, Indiana") is now Business US 20. In [Elkhart County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elkhart_County,_Indiana "Elkhart County, Indiana"), its old route is now classified as a county road and is simply called Old US 20. The [St. Joseph Valley Parkway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Joseph_Valley_Parkway "St. Joseph Valley Parkway") is the present route of US 20 in the South Bend – Elkhart Area.

From 1930 to 1933, US 20 followed what are now [Massachusetts Route 67](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Route_67 "Massachusetts Route 67") and [Massachusetts Route 9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Route_9 "Massachusetts Route 9") from [Worcester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester,_Massachusetts "Worcester, Massachusetts") to [Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palmer,_Massachusetts "Palmer, Massachusetts") via [Shrewsbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrewsbury,_Massachusetts "Shrewsbury, Massachusetts") and [Northborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northborough,_Massachusetts "Northborough, Massachusetts").[[9]](#cite_note-neilbert-9)

The 1953 Massachusetts Department of Public Works Master Plan would have relocated US 20 between [Palmer, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palmer,_Massachusetts "Palmer, Massachusetts") and either [West Springfield, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Springfield,_Massachusetts "West Springfield, Massachusetts") or [Westfield, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westfield,_Massachusetts "Westfield, Massachusetts") along the right-of-way now used by the [Massachusetts Turnpike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Turnpike "Massachusetts Turnpike"), but this was never implemented.[[10]](#cite_note-10)

Until 1970–72,[[9]](#cite_note-neilbert-9) US 20 followed an old route through downtown Springfield—Boston Road and State Street—crossing the Connecticut River via the older [Memorial Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memorial_Bridge_(Massachusetts) "Memorial Bridge (Massachusetts)") (now [Massachusetts Route 147](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Route_147 "Massachusetts Route 147")). The construction of [I-291](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_291_(Massachusetts) "Interstate 291 (Massachusetts)") prompted a change. US 20 now crosses from West Springfield at the North End Bridge, and is co-signed with I-291 until Page Boulevard, where it heads back to Boston Road via the former route of 20A.

In 2012, the Historic US Route 20 Association was formed to identify, promote and preserve the history of the route and original alignments that were once signed "Route 20" from [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston "Boston") to [Newport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newport,_Oregon "Newport, Oregon").[[11]](#cite_note-Historic_US_Route_20-11)

### Historic names

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=U.S._Route_20&action=edit&section=17 "Edit section: Historic names")]

* The portion of US 20 eastward from Illinois generally follows the path of the [Yellowstone Trail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_Trail "Yellowstone Trail").
* In [Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts "Massachusetts"), US 20 follows the [Boston Post Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Post_Road "Boston Post Road") in [Wayland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayland,_Massachusetts "Wayland, Massachusetts") and [Sudbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudbury,_Massachusetts "Sudbury, Massachusetts").
* In the state of [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_(state) "New York (state)"), part of US 20 follows the old [Cherry Valley Turnpike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherry_Valley_Turnpike "Cherry Valley Turnpike") and is still referred to by that name in some places.
* From Buffalo westward to [Cleveland, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleveland "Cleveland"), US 20 follows the path of the Buffalo Stage Road.[[12]](#cite_note-12)

[![](//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b3/Old_Mud_Pike_mile_marker_on_U.S._Route_20_in_Woodville%2C_Ohio.JPG/250px-Old_Mud_Pike_mile_marker_on_U.S._Route_20_in_Woodville%2C_Ohio.JPG?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Old_Mud_Pike_mile_marker_on_U.S._Route_20_in_Woodville,_Ohio.JPG)

One of the 1842 [milestones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milestone "Milestone") marking the original Western Reserve and Maumee Turnpike in Woodville, Ohio.

* Between [Fremont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fremont,_Ohio "Fremont, Ohio"), Ohio and [Perrysburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perrysburg,_Ohio "Perrysburg, Ohio"), Ohio, the road dates back to the late 1830s. It was known then as the Maumee and [Western Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connecticut_Western_Reserve "Connecticut Western Reserve") Road. Nicknamed "Mud Pike" as it cut through the [Great Black Swamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Black_Swamp "Great Black Swamp"), it earned a reputation as "the worst road on the continent" for the mudholes that would trap wagon wheels and slow draft animals. Travelers sometimes would take three days to travel 1 mile (1.6 km). It is still signed as Maumee St. in and near Angola, IN.
* Through western [Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana "Indiana"), US 20 was built as the Dunes Relief Road, as it bypassed the heavily traveled [Dunes Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunes_Highway "Dunes Highway").
* Northwest of [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago "Chicago") it is called Ulysses S. Grant Highway.
* On March 25, 2019, Idaho officially labeled their section of US 20 as the Idaho Medal of Honor Highway in honor of 48 Medal of Honor Recipients with strong Idaho attachments.
* On December 17, 2024, President Biden signed into law Senate bill S.1478, designating the entirety of US 20 as the National Medal of Honor Highway.[[13]](#cite_note-13)
