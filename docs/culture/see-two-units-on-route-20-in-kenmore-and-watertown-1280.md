# See two units on Route 20 in Kenmore and Watertown

**$449,999**

566 COMMONWEALTH AVENUE #304 / **KENMORE**

**SQUARE FEET** 600

**CONDO FEE** $370 a month

**BEDROOMS** 1 **BATHS** 1

**LAST SOLD FOR** $388,000 in 2017

**PROS** This converted studio with parquet floors is on the third floor of Kenmore Towers, a cooperative building in the heart of Kenmore Square, near the Green Line, Boston University, and Fenway Park—and from where Route 20 begins its coast-to-coast course. Enter to find a galley kitchen at left with mosaic tile backsplash; a short hall at right leads to a bath and closet. Past the bedroom—with French doors and transom windows to bring in natural light—the living room has a full wall of southwest-facing floor-to-ceiling windows and sliders to a private balcony. The building has a shared lounge, outdoor pool and hot tub, and common laundry. **CONS** No off-street parking, but with a Walk Score of 9.5 out of 10, a car may not be necessary.
