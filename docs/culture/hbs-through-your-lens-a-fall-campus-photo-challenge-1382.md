# HBS Through Your Lens - A Fall Campus Photo Challenge

Fall Campus Photo Challenge

HBS Through Your Lens
=====================

What does HBS look like through the eyes of the people who experience it every day?

Submit Your Photo

**HBS Through Your Lens** is a community-wide photo challenge open to the greater Harvard community of faculty, staff, students and partners to explore campus, sharpen their photography skills, and share the people, places, details, and moments that make HBS special.

Designed to be accessible and fun—no photography experience or special equipment required—the experience combines a multi-week photo challenge with a community gallery, where the HBS community will vote for their favorite photos.

Challenge Timeline
------------------

Sep 28 - Oct 23

### Look + Capture + Share

The challenge launches and photo submissions open.

Oct 26 - Oct 30

### Community Vote

The finalist gallery opens for people’s choice voting.

Nov 9 - Nov 20

### Celebrate

Winners are announced, and selected photographs are showcased online and in Spangler Lounge.
